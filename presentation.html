Group A 6 problems

The engine decides, so the customer doesn't have to

P1 Which devices work together P2 Matching needs to devices P4 Which products suit me P6 Seeing all the options P10 Will it work in my house P24 The right system for my budget

Three states instead of yes or no. Compatible, compatible with a stated limit, or greyed out with the reason in one sentence and the missing part on an add button.

Nobody picks devices. The tool proposes Essential, Comfort and Premium, built from composition rules: no thermostat, one in the living room, or one per room.

Budget is shown, not applied as a filter. A level above budget stays visible and greyed, with the gap written next to it.

Live demo 2 min
  1. Landing page → Demo scenario, mixed house in one click.
  2. On the result screen, open Other choice on a line, then pick a device that needs a gateway: greyed, with the reason and the add UG800 button.
  3. Move the budget slider down — Premium stays on screen with the gap.
SayThe third state is the one that matters. A customer never hits a dead end, they get a sentence and a button. Also mention the completeness check: the whole basket is tested, so a missing wiring centre is caught even if nobody clicked anything.
Group B 5 problems

The shape of the journey

P3 Unclear journey P5 Not intuitive P7 Too many steps before buying P11 Online or offline P23 Starting over every time

One path, four named steps. My home, my heating, my habits, my solution. The bar is always visible and going back costs nothing.

The two hardest questions are never asked. "Do I need connected?" becomes a conclusion after three questions about daily life. "What will it cost?" appears as a running total from step two.

A project is never lost, only paused. A project code from the first answer, the exact step restored, and a resume link by email — the same email that feeds the CRM.

Live demo 2 min

  2. Show the running total appearing at the end of "My heating".
  3. Answer the three habit questions → the connected conclusion and the comparison card, shown once.
  4. Reload the page → the project code brings the answers back.

Group C 8 problems — the biggest group

One configuration, eight outputs

P12 Why remote access helps P17 Hard to picture the system P18 Working out the total price P19 Estimating the savings P20 No step-by-step guide P21 Benefits poorly explained P22 Comparing the options P16 No guided videos

Nothing is written in advance. The diagram, the room-by-room benefits, the savings and the installation guide are all generated from the same configuration.

So the quote, the guide and the drawing can never disagree — and the customer never re-enters anything to get them.

Savings come out as a range, from four inputs the customer already knows, with the method readable on screen.

Group D 4 problems

The installer is a second user

P8 Having to be on site to specify P13 Drowning in documentation P14 Finding a replacement device P25 Turning a configuration into a quote

Arrive prepared. A pre-visit link goes to the customer, who answers the home questions and sends three photos we ask for by name: heat source, radiator with its valve, electrical panel.

Only the documents that matter. The matrix is queried with the selected products, and a document that does not exist is shown as missing instead of hidden.

The quote protects the distributor. Retail price with the exact product codes, plus the line "present this quote to your Salus distributor". No net price in the tool.

Group E 3 problems — least code, most value

When the tool can't decide, and how it learns

P9 No single supplier for everything P15 Market configurators are too limited P26 Not connected to the CRM

The limit becomes a handover, not a refusal. Commercial building, more than 12 zones, a heat source we don't cover: the tool keeps the part of the recommendation it could build, prepares the file and promises a call back within 48 hours.

A configuration is a qualified lead from the first answer — profile, postcode, project, zones, level, amount, and the step where the customer stopped.

The loop that keeps it from ageing. Every correction a sales rep makes by hand is a rule the engine was missing. That is the only feedback loop in the system, and it is the one worth having.

Live demo 1–2 min
  1. Open the CRM backstage panel: the Lead, Quote and Deal payloads, the event log, what the rep sees next.
  2. Trigger an out-of-scope project to show the handover message.
SayThis is the commercial slide. Even if the rest stayed a demo, a configurator that creates a qualified lead at the first answer pays for itself. Nothing is connected today — the panel shows exactly what would be sent.

Prices

Fictional and labelled on every line. Production takes the recommended retail price from the ERP list.

Documents

Real PDFs generated from the configuration, with a TEST DOCUMENT watermark and fictional content.

Zoho CRM

Not connected. Nothing leaves the browser. The backstage panel shows the exact payloads.

References

Real product codes, except three still marked "ref. to confirm".
